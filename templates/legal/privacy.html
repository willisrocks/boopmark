Boopmark

Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

What Boopmark handles

  • Account information: information used to identify your Boopmark account, such as your email address and profile details when you sign in on the web.
  • Bookmark content: URLs, titles, notes, tags, images, and related metadata that you choose to save.
  • Connection information: API credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain or Chrome's extension-local storage on your device. Boopmark servers receive the credential when authenticating API requests, but Boopmark does not send it to advertising or analytics services.
  • Operational information: standard server logs and diagnostics needed to keep the service secure and reliable.

Chrome extension

The Boopmark Chrome extension reads the active tab's URL and title only when you open the extension. It does not read page contents, cookies, or browsing history and does not collect data while it is closed. The extension sends the selected URL and the bookmark fields you review to your configured Boopmark server for metadata lookup and saving. If AI is enabled in your Boopmark account, that server may automatically use its configured AI provider to suggest a title, description, and tags. Your Boopmark API key is stored locally by Chrome and sent only to the Boopmark server you choose.

How information is used

We use this information only to provide and protect the service: authenticating you, saving and syncing bookmarks, generating previews, offering search, and troubleshooting failures. Boopmark does not sell personal information, track you across other companies’ apps or websites, or serve behavioral advertising.

Service providers

Boopmark uses infrastructure providers to host the application, database, and bookmark images. When AI features are enabled or you request an AI action, the bookmark URL and extracted page content needed for that feature may be processed by your configured provider, Anthropic or OpenAI. Sites and metadata services contacted to create a bookmark preview can also receive the saved URL as part of that request. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Retention and control

Bookmark data remains until you delete it or the associated account is removed. You can delete bookmarks in the app or web interface and revoke mobile API keys from Boopmark settings. Short-lived backups and security logs may remain for a limited period where needed for service integrity or legal compliance.

Security and self-hosting

Traffic to the hosted Boopmark service uses HTTPS. The iOS app stores its API key using Apple’s Keychain, and the Chrome extension uses Chrome's extension-local storage. If you connect an app or extension to a self-hosted Boopmark server, that server’s operator controls its data practices and is responsible for its own privacy disclosures and security.

Children and changes

Boopmark is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when the product or legal requirements change; the effective date above will be updated when that happens.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions, data-access requests, or account-deletion help, visit the Boopmark support page.
