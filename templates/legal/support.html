Boopmark Support

Help with the iPhone app, Share Extension, and hosted or self-hosted Boopmark servers.

Connect the iPhone app

  1. Sign in to your Boopmark server in a web browser.
  2. Open Settings and create an API key.
  3. In the iPhone app, open Settings and enter the HTTPS server URL and API key.
  4. Tap Save connection.

Save from another app

Open the system Share Sheet in Safari or another app, choose Boopmark, review the title, note, and tags, then tap Save. If Boopmark is not visible, choose More in the Share Sheet and enable it.

Offline captures

When a network request cannot reach the server, Boopmark can keep the capture on your device. Open Settings and use Send queued captures after reconnecting. Sending is intentionally manual to avoid duplicate bookmarks.

Get more help

Report a problem or request account-deletion help through Boopmark’s public support tracker. Do not include API keys, passwords, private bookmark URLs, or other confidential information in a public issue.

See also the Boopmark Privacy Policy.
